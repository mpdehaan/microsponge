# SciFi

SciFi is a later theme, inspired by Stargate: many planets, each a small world of its own, space
between them, and stargates, warp portals between planets. Only its skeleton is in the code.
[GUIDE.md](GUIDE.md) says how every theme is played; this page says what SciFi is like, and grows
as the theme does.

## What SciFi is like

- A game starts with 1 to 50 planets, and the person setting up the game chooses how many. Units
  that are not space units are stuck on their planet, and cities are on the planets.
- Space is a layer above the planets: space units orbit, fight each other, and may bombard a
  planet. A space fighter defends the space around a planet, and a space carrier carries space
  fighters between planets.
- A stargate is a high technology that lets giant ships pass between planets at once; without
  one, the voyage is long and slow.
- Anti space missiles and anti space lasers on the ground shoot at large space units.
- The units include at least infantry, the probe, the space fighter and the space carrier, and
  the buildings the stargate and the spaceport.
