# The theme called fantasy

What the user has decided about fantasy, a theme of dragons and magic with a layer like space
called the astral plane. The model, `GAME_RULES.md`, says how the game works for every theme and
what a theme may decide; this file holds what the theme itself chooses. `CLAUDE.md` imports this
file, so it's in force in every session.

Nothing is added here that the user hasn't said (`.claude/COMMUNICATION.md`, "Who decides what").
Classic is being developed first; this theme is recorded so that the engine allows for it.

## What the user has said

- Fantasy has a layer that is kind of like space, called the astral plane, which only works
  for the current planet: fantasy has one world. The properties of the astral plane are
  undefined for now, but some units can move between the real world and the astral plane.
- The units are at least these: dragons, knights, wyverns, monks, clerics, rangers, sorcerers,
  wizards, elementals, soldiers, scouts, settlers, workers, ogres, goblins and blimps. Psychic
  units can interact with the astral plane. Which units are psychic is not decided.
- The tech tree is to be determined on dependencies, and includes at least: dragon husbandry,
  dragon riding, chivalry, wyvern husbandry, magic, fire magic, water magic, earth magic, air
  magic, elementals, necromancy, psionics, teleportation, spirituality, nature attunement,
  guilds, summoning, telepathy and blimps.
- The units and the technology tree of Warcraft II may be looked at a bit for inspiration, as
  a homage, later. As with every other game, Claude asks before any idea from Warcraft II goes
  in, and says that the idea comes from Warcraft II.

## What Claude chose, to be confirmed

- In the code, dragons, wyverns and blimps are air units and every other unit is a land unit,
  so that the stub loads. Which class of unit each really is, and what each can do, is the
  user's to say.

## Not decided yet

- The properties of the astral plane, and which units move between the real world and the
  astral plane.
- What each unit can do, which units are psychic, and what each technology depends on and
  allows.
- The buildings, the resources and the terrains.
