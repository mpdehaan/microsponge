# The theme called SciFi

What the user has decided about SciFi, a theme inspired by Stargate, with many planets, space
between them and warp portals. The theme was first called SPACE, and was renamed because space
itself is a feature of the engine that classic uses in a limited way ("Space" in the model). The
model, `GAME_RULES.md`, says how the game works for every theme and what a theme may decide, under
"Worlds", "Space" and "Visibility" for this one; this file holds what the theme itself chooses.
`CLAUDE.md` imports this file, so it's in force in every session.

Nothing is added here that the user hasn't said (`.claude/COMMUNICATION.md`, "Who decides what").
Classic is being developed first; this theme is recorded so that the engine allows for it.

## What the user has said

- The theme has different planets, and the grid of a planet could be small.
- A game can start with multiple planets: at least 1 planet and at most 50, for now, and the
  person setting up the game chooses how many.
- Units that are not space units are stuck on their planet, and cities are on the maps of the
  planets.
- Space is a layer, as the model says under "Space": space units orbit planets, are built at a
  spaceport or a starport, attack each other, and may bombard a planet.
- Stargates are warp portals between planets. A stargate can either be discovered, or maybe
  built, and is a high technology item. A stargate supports the transport of giant ships
  between planets; without one, a giant ship takes a long, slow voyage.
- The space fighter is the smaller space ship, which only does space defense around a planet,
  and the space carrier is the unit that transports space fighters between planets.
- There is also interdimensional space travel, so there is a way to jump between planets.
- In space, a planet can be attacked with the appropriate technology.
- There are rules about whether a ship can land on a planet or not.
- The planets are distributed in a way similar to how the world builder makes a world.
- Certain technology automatically reveals the land, and also the map, depending on what the
  user decides.
- Stealth units may be allowed.
- The buildings include at least the stargate, and the spaceport, the anti space missiles and
  the anti space lasers that the model describes. The details are to be determined.
- The units include at least infantry, the probe, the space fighter and the space carrier.
  The details are to be determined.
- The buildings that add research points may be different from the buildings classic uses.
- Crossing the poles of a world may require a certain level of technology, only when the world
  has ice caps, and a civilization may start with that level depending on where in time the
  game starts.
