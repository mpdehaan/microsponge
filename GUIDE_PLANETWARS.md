# PlanetWars

PlanetWars is a theoretical theme, a template for a game in which every civilization starts alone
on a planet of its own, only big enough for one city, and has to cross space to meet anyone. Only
its skeleton is in the code. [GUIDE.md](GUIDE.md) says how every theme is played; this page says
what PlanetWars is like, and grows as the theme does.

## The story

To be written, with the person who designs the game.

## What PlanetWars is like

- Everybody starts with one city on a planet of their own, and there may be empty planets.
- A colony ship that reaches a world can spawn a settler there, and a space transport can land
  units, though the anti space defenses of a planet may shoot a transport down.
- The units include at least the settler, the colony ship and the space transport.
