# Fantasy

Fantasy is a later theme of dragons and magic, with a layer like space called the astral plane,
which some units can cross into. Only its skeleton is in the code. [GUIDE.md](GUIDE.md) says how
every theme is played; this page says what fantasy is like, and grows as the theme does.

## The story

To be written, with the person who designs the game.

## What fantasy is like

- Fantasy has one world and the astral plane above it, which psychic units can reach.
- The units are dragons, knights, wyverns, monks, clerics, rangers, sorcerers, wizards,
  elementals, soldiers, scouts, settlers, workers, ogres, goblins and blimps.
- The tech tree includes dragon husbandry, dragon riding, chivalry, wyvern husbandry, magic,
  fire magic, water magic, earth magic, air magic, elementals, necromancy, psionics,
  teleportation, spirituality, nature attunement, guilds, summoning, telepathy and blimps.
