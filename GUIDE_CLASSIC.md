# Classic

Classic is the first real theme of microsponge: historic earth in the style of a civilization
game, from the stone age to the internet. It is being designed now, and only its skeleton is in
the code: the names of its units, buildings, technologies and world wonders, and the first few
numbers. [GUIDE.md](GUIDE.md) says how every theme is played; this page says what classic is like,
and grows as classic does.

## What classic is like

- The resources are currency, food, wood, stone and metal. Metal is lumped together, comes from
  the mountains that have a metal deposit, and a mine gives more of it. Iron, gold, uranium and
  silk are strategic resources: a civilization that has one within its borders can build what
  requires it, and a legion requires iron.
- Desert gives no food, and once irrigation is known a desert tile next to a river or a lake can
  be turned into grassland.
- The units run from the settler, the scout and the warrior through the phalanx, the legion,
  the catapult and the knights to the musketeer, the cannon, the howitzer, infantry, fighter
  planes, bombers, the stealth bomber, cruise and nuclear missiles, drones and the anti-missile
  and anti-air platforms. A legion is about 4 times a warrior, a musketeer 5 times a legion, and
  infantry 5 times a musketeer. The catapult, the cannon and the howitzer do not strike back,
  bypass city walls, and each has one hit point.
- City walls are researched with fortification and cost stone. A bank raises a city's income by
  20 percent. Libraries, colleges and research centers give research points.
- The world wonders are the pyramids, the great wall, the manhattan project, which unlocks
  nuclear weapons, the library of alexandria, and the rest of the seven wonders of the world.
- A city can launch up to three satellites into the space around Earth.
